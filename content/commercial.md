Commercial work is a different trade to domestic, even when the equipment is
the same. The decision is made by someone who will never see the plant room,
against a budget set nine months earlier, and the question is almost never
"can you fix it" — it is "what will this cost me across the next five years,
and when."

**What we are good at.** Small and mid-sized sites in the Driftless region
where a national contractor will not send a van for less than a day rate.
Chapel halls, small manufacturing, farm buildings that became offices, the
county's older municipal stock.

**What we are not.** We do not hold 24/7 SLAs, we do not have a night desk,
and we will not pretend otherwise to win a tender. Our emergency cover is a
rota of people who live here, which is better than a call centre most nights
and worse than a national on Christmas Eve.

**How we price it.** Per site, after a walk round, net 30 against a PO. Every
commercial quote we ever gave over the phone was wrong, and we stopped in
2019.

Condition reports carry a replace-by year. That is the whole reason to hold a
contract with us rather than call whoever answers — you get to plan.
