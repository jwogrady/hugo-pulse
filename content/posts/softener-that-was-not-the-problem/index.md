title = 'The Softener That Was Not the Problem'
date = 2026-01-19T17:10:00-06:00
summary = 'Two callbacks on a softener that worked perfectly. The pressure tank had been dead for a year.'
tags = ['well water', 'callbacks']
+++

Customer reported the softener had stopped working — hard water at every tap,
salt going down normally. Second opinion; someone else had already swapped the
control head.

The softener was fine. So was the new head.

What was wrong was the pressure tank. The bladder had failed, so the pump was
short-cycling every few seconds, and the softener never saw a steady enough
flow to complete a regeneration cycle. It was going through the motions and
backwashing into a system that kept dropping pressure underneath it.

The tell was audible from the yard and I should have heard it before I opened
the softener cabinet. Twelve minutes of listening would have saved the
customer a control head.

Replaced the tank, repressurised to 2 psi below cut-in, and the softener has
been fine since.
