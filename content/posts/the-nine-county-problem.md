Furthest corner of the patch is ninety minutes each way. On a well test that
is three hours of driving against forty minutes of work.

Two ways to handle that. Hide it in the price everybody pays, so the customer
eight minutes away subsidises the one in the far corner. Or charge mileage and
watch people in the far corner go without testing.

What we actually do is batch. Far-corner work gets scheduled together, we go
out one day a fortnight, and the price is the same everywhere. It means a
non-urgent job out there might wait twelve days.

We tell people that when they book, and almost nobody minds. What they mind is
finding out on the day.
