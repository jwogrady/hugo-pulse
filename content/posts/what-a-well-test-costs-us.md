Lab fee is $64 for bacteriological and nitrate. Courier to get it there cold
and inside hold time is $18, and that is the part nobody costs in.

Then the visit: forty minutes on site if the tap is accessible, longer if we
have to find one that is plumbed before the treatment system. Drive time
across this patch averages fifty minutes each way.

At $180 the margin is thin and it is meant to be. The test is how we find the
work, and a test priced to make money on its own is a test people skip.

The $25 kit from the hardware store is a different product. It tests at the
tap, after treatment, with no hold time control and no chain of custody. It
will tell you something. It will not tell you whether your well is safe, and
no lender or health department will accept it.
