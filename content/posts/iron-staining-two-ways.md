Both customers described it identically: orange staining in the bath, worse in
the morning.

The first was ferrous iron — dissolved, water clear at the tap and colouring
up on standing as it oxidises. That one takes a softener or an oxidising
filter depending on how much of it there is.

The second was ferric — already oxidised in the aquifer, water visibly tinted
straight out of the tap. That is a filtration problem, not an ion exchange
one, and a softener would have loaded up and failed inside a year.

You cannot tell them apart by the stain. You can tell them apart by filling a
clear glass and watching it for twenty minutes, and you can tell them apart
properly with a test that distinguishes the two.
