Inherited a system where someone had fitted UV straight onto a well with
visible sediment and no pre-filtration. Lamp lit, flow normal, customer
confident.

UV disinfects what the light reaches. Particulates shadow bacteria, and
anything riding on a grain of sediment passes through untouched. The
installation had been there four years and the customer had stopped boiling
water because of it.

Sediment filter, then a five-micron, then the lamp. And a test afterwards,
because the only way to know a disinfection step works is to sample after it.
