Commercial unit, twenty-six years old, tenant chapel hall with a full
congregation on Sundays.

First visit: failed ignition control. Part was still available, $290, back in
service the same afternoon. Second visit five weeks later: heat exchanger
weeping at a seam, which is the end of the line for that model.

The second fault was visible on the first visit. There was surface corrosion
at the seam and I logged it as "monitor" because condemning a boiler in
January on a building with no alternative heat is a conversation nobody wants
to start.

That was the wrong call. The customer spent $290 on a unit that had five weeks
left, and the replacement then happened in a hurry, in the cold, at worse
prices than if we had started the conversation in the first week.

Condition reports now say "replace within" with a date on them, or they say
nothing.
