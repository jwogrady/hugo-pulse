We stopped doing it in 2019 and I have gone back through why.

Fourteen phone quotes that year on treatment systems. Eleven came in over,
averaging 38% over. Two came in roughly right. One came in under, and that one
was under because we did the job at the quoted price and ate the difference.

It is not that the customer describes it badly. It is that the questions that
change the price — what the test says, where the discharge goes, whether there
is a power outlet within reach, whether the crawlspace is a crawlspace or a
gap — are not questions a customer knows they are answering.

A site visit costs us about an hour. Being 38% wrong costs more than that, and
it costs it in the conversation where you explain the number went up.
