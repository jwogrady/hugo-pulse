Called out to a farmhouse near Gays Mills, no water upstairs, minus eighteen
overnight. The run was 3/4" copper through an uninsulated crawlspace, about
nine metres of it, and the cold end had let go at a coupling.

Thawed it slowly with heat mats rather than a torch, replaced the coupling,
and wrapped the run in pipe lagging. Two hours, $340 in labour and fittings.

Three weeks later it froze again, further along.

The lagging was never going to work. It slows heat loss out of the pipe; it
does nothing about a crawlspace sitting at ambient. What that run needed was
either self-regulating trace heat or rerouting above the insulation line, and
I knew that on the first visit and did the cheap thing anyway because the
customer was standing there without water.

Went back and put trace heat on it at our cost.
