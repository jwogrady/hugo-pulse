**Call (608) 555-0117.** Someone picks up, day or night.

**Turn the water off first** if water is the problem. The stop tap is usually
under the kitchen sink or where the supply enters the building. If you cannot
find it, say so on the phone — we will talk you through it before anyone sets
off, because five minutes on the phone beats forty minutes of driving.

**If it is heat**, and the building is occupied and below 10°C, that is an
emergency and we treat it as one. If it is above that and there is another
heat source, it will usually cost you less to wait for a morning booking, and
we will say so.

We carry enough on the van to stop the water and restore heat. The permanent
repair is normally the next working day, in daylight, with the parts house
open — once, rather than twice.
