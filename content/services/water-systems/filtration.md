We will not quote a treatment system without a current test. Anyone who does
is selling you equipment, not treating your water.

**Softening** for hardness above roughly 120 mg/L, where scale is costing you
a water heater every few years.

**Iron and manganese removal** where the staining is the complaint. The
method depends on the form the iron is in, which is why the test comes first.

**UV** for bacteriological failures, and only on water that is already clear —
UV through cloudy water is a light bill, not a disinfectant.

Annual servicing is not optional on any of these. A neglected system is worse
than none, because you stop boiling the water.
