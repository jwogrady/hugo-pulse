Once a year, and after any work on the well, the pump, or the pressure tank.

The sample matters more than the lab. We draw from a fixed interior tap after
a timed flush, sterilise the spout, and get it cold to the lab inside the
hold time. A sample taken off a garden hose tells you about the hose.

Results come back in five to seven working days with the actual numbers, not
a pass mark. If it fails we will tell you whether the fix is a shock
chlorination, a treatment system, or a conversation about a new well.
