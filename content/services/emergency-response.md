You call, someone picks up. Not a service, not a queue — whoever is on rota
that night.

We carry enough on the van to stop the water and restore heat. A permanent
repair usually happens the next working day, because the parts house is shut
and we would rather do it once in daylight than twice in the dark.

**What this covers.** Burst or frozen supply lines, failed boilers and
furnaces below 10°C indoors, sewage backing up, no water to an occupied
building.

**What it does not.** Blocked single fixtures, appliances, anything cosmetic.
Those wait for a normal booking and cost less.

No fixed price. An emergency call is time plus parts, billed at the rate we
quote you on the phone before anyone gets in a van.
