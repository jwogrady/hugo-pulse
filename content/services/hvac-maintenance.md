Two visits a year, spring and autumn. We service what is there rather than
recommending what is not.

**On a contract.** Two scheduled visits, filters and belts included, priority
booking in a cold snap, and a written condition report each visit that says
what will need replacing and roughly when.

**One-off.** The same visit without the priority or the report discount.

Contract pricing depends on plant size and how many buildings. We walk the
site before quoting, because every quote given over the phone has been wrong.
