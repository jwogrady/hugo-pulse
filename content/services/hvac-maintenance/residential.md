You are buying a furnace that starts in November.

**The visit.** About ninety minutes. Burners, heat exchanger, flue, filter,
blower, thermostat calibration, and a carbon monoxide check at the register
whether or not you asked for one.

**What it costs.** $165 a visit. $290 if you book the autumn and spring
visits together, which is not a discount so much as us not driving out twice
to book the second one.

**What we will tell you.** If the unit has under two years left, we will say
so, with a number, so you can plan rather than replace in an emergency at
whatever price is available that week.

No contract, no autorenewal. We will call you in September; you can say no.
