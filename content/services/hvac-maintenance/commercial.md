You are buying predictability, not repairs.

**The contract.** Two scheduled visits a year across all plant on site.
Filters and belts included. Priority booking when the weather turns, which is
the clause that actually matters — in a February cold snap we are triaging,
and contract sites go first.

**The report.** Every visit produces a written condition report naming what
will need replacing and the year it will need replacing in. It exists so you
can put a number in next year's budget instead of finding out in January.
Since 2026 those reports carry a replace-by date rather than the word
"monitor", because "monitor" is how a boiler gets repaired five weeks before
it is condemned.

**Pricing.** Per site, set by plant count and building count, quoted after a
walk round. We do not quote commercial work over the phone.

Invoicing is net 30 against a PO if you need one.
