Ray Meridian took the first call in March 1987, from a farmhouse outside
Soldiers Grove with no heat and a foot of snow. He fixed it, badly, and went
back the following week to fix it properly at his own cost.

That is more or less the whole strategy. Three vans by 1994, the water side
added in 2003 when the well work stopped being something we subcontracted,
and commercial maintenance after that because the farms we serviced kept
buying buildings.

We have never advertised. Every customer came from someone who already had
our number.
