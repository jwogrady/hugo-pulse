Dale decides who goes where, which in a nine-county patch in February is most
of the job.

He came in on the domestic side in 2004, moved across to water systems when
we took that in-house, and holds the accreditations that let us sign off
private supply work. If you have had a well tested by us in the last decade,
Dale either drew the sample or trained whoever did.

He is also the reason we stopped quoting treatment systems over the phone.
